Vulnerability assessment and controlled exposure reduction.

CyberShield discovers hosts and services on networks you are authorized to assess, correlates detected software against CVE records, and — for eligible laboratory targets — applies reversible, network-level isolation that it then verifies by re-scanning. It is fail-closed: nothing is scanned unless you have explicitly authorized it.

⌖

Real Nmap scanning

Host discovery, service and version detection through curated, non-intrusive Nmap profiles. An unreachable target is recorded as unreachable — never as a clean result.

⚠

CVE correlation

Detected software is normalized to CPEs and correlated against NVD. Every match is a candidate with its evidence, confidence and CVSS score — reviewed by a human, not auto-confirmed.

⛨

Controlled remediation

For eligible lab targets, reversible firewall isolation with the rollback command stored before it is applied, a TTL auto-revert, and protected management ports that can never be cut off.

▦

Professional reports

Per-scan HTML and PDF reports covering targets, services, CVE candidates, severity, remediation status and verification — with the scan's real state stated plainly.

≡

Tamper-evident audit

Every scan, review and remediation is written to a hash-chained audit log. You see your own trail; the chain's integrity is verifiable end to end.

◐

Isolated by account

Each operator's targets, scans, findings, reports and audit are private to their account. A new account starts with a clean, empty dashboard.

How it works

Authorize a target

Add a private IPv4 address or range you own or have written permission to assess. Public ranges are refused.

Run a scan

Pick a profile and scan. Scope and authorization are re-checked before a single packet is sent.

Correlate & review

Correlate services against NVD, then review the CVE candidates with their evidence and confidence.

Reduce & verify

Where eligible, apply reversible isolation and confirm the change by re-scanning — then generate a report.

Security-first by design

CyberShield reduces network exposure; it does not patch software, and it never claims to. A CVE correlation is a candidate requiring human review, not a confirmed vulnerability. Firewall actions are reversible, require explicit confirmation, and are refused on any target that is not an authorized, remediable laboratory host. This is a tool for authorized assessment of your own systems and lab environments — not for scanning networks you do not control.
